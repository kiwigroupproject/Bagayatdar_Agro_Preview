Who We Are

A trusted second-generation agro-food business

Bagayatdar Agro Foods is a trusted second-generation agro-food business offering premium-quality Goa Cashew, Cow Ghee, Dry Fruits, Legumes, Sprouts, and Bakery Products across India. As a reliable B2B and B2C supplier, we are committed to delivering fresh, hygienically packed, and quality-assured products at competitive prices.

With years of industry experience, we serve retailers, wholesalers, distributors, businesses, and individual customers with consistent quality and dependable service. Whether you're looking for healthy daily essentials or bulk agro-food supplies, Bagayatdar Agro Foods is your trusted partner for premium agro products.

We proudly provide PAN India delivery, ensuring quality, freshness, and customer satisfaction in every order.
